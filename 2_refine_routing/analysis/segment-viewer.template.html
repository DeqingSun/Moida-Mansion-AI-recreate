Geometry, R & C assumptions

Source: Moida-routing_to_vendor.dxf. Both COM and SEG contacts are at the bottom. The source clamps the outer 1.5 mm of each 1 × 3 mm pad. The artwork is shown from the front; the rear COM geometry is overlaid without mirroring.

R assumes ITO sheet resistance of 30 Ω/□. Values are estimates from a 0.025 mm sheet mesh; the sampled mesh refinement change is shown below. Zebra connector, PCB and driver resistance are excluded. C is area × the editable assumed density. COM/SEG totals include full opposite-layer ITO overlap; island C uses visible area. OFF islands still load the drivers. COM and SEG totals count the same capacitors from opposite sides; do not add them. Fringing and external stray C are excluded. Density depends on the LC material and state; no measured specification is available. The 10 pF/mm² baseline follows NXP AN11491 §4.3; 30 is a sensitivity case, not a bound.

First / last refer to shortest / longest connected path to an island, not minimum / maximum resistance. R is available for those endpoints only. The selected island is treated as an equipotential terminal; other islands remain finite resistive sheets.

All geometry and data are embedded in this file. No network or backend is used.
