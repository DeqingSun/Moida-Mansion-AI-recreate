How to route
  1. Select a segment and assign its top and bottom net names.
  2. Choose a layer, routing net and trace width.
  3. Draw from an island toward a pad. Click bends; double-click or Enter to finish. Endpoints snap to pads and trace vertices.
  4. Touched islands take the trace’s net unless another net’s trace touches them. A floating net routed to an edge pad adopts that pad’s net. Top is red; bottom is blue.
  5. Use Erase to remove a whole trace, or select one and press Delete. Undo restores it.
  6. Run checks. Fix missing islands, duplicate pairs between different original groups, shorts and overlaps outside the original shapes.
  7. Save JSON to keep an editable project. Local autosave also preserves the latest layout in this browser.

Space-drag or middle-drag pans; wheel zooms. V selects, R draws, E erases, H pans. Ctrl/Cmd Z undoes; Shift Z or Ctrl Y redoes. Ctrl/Cmd S saves; O loads. Loading and New are undoable.
